Privacy Policy

rclone Workspace Sync is a private, owner-operated utility. It is not a public service and does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Google user data accessed

The utility uses the drive.file scope to create, read, and update only Google Drive files created by the utility or explicitly opened with it. Access is used solely to synchronize the owner's selected workspace files between the owner's computers.

Storage and processing

File contents remain in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's devices. OAuth tokens are stored only in private rclone configuration on those devices. This website has no application server, database, analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Sharing and disclosure

The utility does not disclose Google user data to third parties. Google Drive processes files as the storage provider under the owner's Google account and Google's applicable terms.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention by deleting files from Google Drive or the local devices. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings, and local OAuth tokens can be removed from rclone configuration.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project issue tracker.
